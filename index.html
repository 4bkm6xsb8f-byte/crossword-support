How to play

Fill the 5×5 grid using the across and down clues. Tap a square to select it and tap again to switch direction. The faster you solve, the better your time.

Frequently asked

When does the daily puzzle change? There is one shared daily puzzle, the same for everyone. Puzzle #1 was 1 November 2026, and a new one arrives each day on your device's local calendar day.

What are Unlimited puzzles? Generated puzzles you can play as often as you like. They don't count toward your day streak.

How do day streaks work? Finish the daily puzzle on its day and your streak goes up by one. Miss a day and it resets. Finishing with help (see below) still counts and keeps your streak.

What do Check and Reveal do? Check marks any wrong letters in the grid. Reveal fills in squares for you. Using either counts the solve as "with help": the puzzle still finishes and your day streak is kept, but that time stays off the Game Center leaderboard.

Do my history and settings sync between devices? Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud, your history and settings sync through your own private iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account and come back if you reinstall. We can't see that data. With iCloud off, everything stays on the device and the game works the same.

How do Game Center leaderboards and achievements work? If you're signed in to Game Center, your unaided solve times go to the leaderboards and you can earn achievements. Sign in through Settings > Game Center on your device. The game plays fine without it.

Can I get a daily reminder? Yes, it's optional. Turn it on in the app's Settings and choose a time; iOS will ask for notification permission the first time. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device, and you can turn it off at any time.

Does FH Crossword collect my data? No. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking, and nothing is sent to FH Development Studio. See our Privacy Policy.
