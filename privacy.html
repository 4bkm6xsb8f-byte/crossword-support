FH Crossword

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

FH Development Studio ("we," "us") built FH Crossword as a 5×5 mini crossword game with no accounts and no servers of our own. This policy is short because the app does very little with data.

What we collect

Nothing. FH Crossword has no user accounts, includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and sends nothing to FH Development Studio. We operate no servers for this app, so there is nothing for us to receive.

What's stored on your device

Your puzzle history and statistics (which puzzles you played, whether you finished them, your solve times, whether you used Check or Reveal, and your day streak) are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your settings are stored on your device and sync only through your own iCloud account, as described below.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your history and settings also sync through your own private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage. That means they reach your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, and they come back if you reinstall the app. This data:

  • Lives in your private iCloud account, under your own Apple Account
  • Is not visible or accessible to FH Development Studio, and we have no way to read it
  • Is never sent to any server run by us or by any third party
  • Is handled by Apple under Apple's own iCloud privacy practices, not ours

If you're not signed in to iCloud, or iCloud is unavailable for the app, everything stays on the device and the game works exactly the same — sync is the only thing you lose.

Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. Removing the data held in your iCloud account is done through Apple's own iCloud settings, since it's your account, not ours.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app submits your solve times and achievements to Apple's Game Center. That information is handled by Apple under Apple's own Game Center privacy practices; it is not sent to us. The game works without Game Center.

Daily reminder

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It is not sent through any server, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Third parties

FH Crossword uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks of any kind. The only outside services involved are Apple's iCloud and Game Center, acting on your account.

Children's privacy

FH Crossword does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, for the same reason stated above: it collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will change accordingly.
